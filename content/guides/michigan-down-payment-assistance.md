The most widely available down payment help in Michigan comes from **MSHDA**
— the Michigan State Housing Development Authority. It's a state agency,
not a lender itself, but it works through participating lenders to offer
loans with built-in down payment assistance.

## How it actually works

MSHDA's assistance isn't a grant you keep for free. It's structured as a
**second loan** on top of your main mortgage — up to $10,000 in most of
the state, or up to $12,500 in specific targeted areas — that covers your
down payment and/or closing costs.

That second loan has **0% interest and no monthly payment**. It becomes
due when you sell the home, refinance, or pay off the first mortgage —
whichever comes first.

## Who qualifies

- You're purchasing a home in Michigan (primary residence — not an
  investment property or second home)
- Your household income falls under MSHDA's limit for your county and
  household size (limits vary — see the income limits guide)
- The home's sale price is under MSHDA's purchase price limit for that
  area
- You meet the minimum credit score requirement set by the specific loan
  program you're using (this varies by loan type — FHA, conventional, USDA,
  RD, or VA — since MSHDA assistance pairs with an underlying first
  mortgage)

Unlike some state programs, **MSHDA doesn't require you to be a
first-time buyer** for most of its programs — that's a common
misconception. The MI Home Loan Flex program specifically allows repeat
buyers.

## What it actually covers

- Down payment
- Closing costs
- Or a combination of both

## The tradeoff to understand

Because it's a loan, not a grant, it does add to what you technically owe
against the home. It won't show up as a monthly payment, but it will
reduce how much you walk away with if you sell before paying off both
loans. For many buyers, especially those who'd otherwise struggle to save
a down payment at all, that tradeoff is well worth it — but it's worth
understanding going in rather than assuming it's free money.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I have to pay MSHDA assistance back in monthly installments?**
No — it has no monthly payment. It's repaid in full when you sell,
refinance, or pay off your first mortgage.

**Can I combine MSHDA assistance with an FHA loan?**
Yes — MSHDA down payment assistance can pair with FHA, conventional,
USDA/RD, or VA first mortgages, as long as you meet that program's
requirements.

**Is there a deadline to apply?**
MSHDA funding is allocated on an ongoing basis rather than a fixed
annual deadline, but availability can shift, so it's worth confirming
current status when you're ready to apply rather than assuming it's
always open.
